The January cup

Straight knockout, random draw

Straight knockout, drawn at random. Your gameweek score is your score in the tie — highest wins, and if it's level the higher league position goes through.

The draw

With the box ticked, the byes go to whoever is highest in the league when you draw it, and everyone else is pulled out at random — so a bye is earned rather than lucky. Untick it for a straight random draw. Rounds are scored from the January gameweeks in order. Anyone can look; only whoever runs the league should press it.

Where the numbers come from

Updated automatically, every hour

A job runs every hour, reads the official Fantasy Draft API and writes the scores this page reads. Nobody has to do anything, and the link never changes. Refresh at the top pulls the newest file straight away.

Changing the rules

config.json

Prize money, the gameweeks each month covers, and the agreed first and second round picks all live in config.json. Edit that file and everything below retotals on the next update.

The star player names for December are the only thing that cannot be fetched — they go in the same file.
